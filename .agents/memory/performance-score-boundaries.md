---
name: Performance score boundaries
description: Product and methodological guardrails for the central APH performance score.
---

The APH Leistungs-Score is a performance-only, transparent summary of COD,
Sprint, Power, Ausdauer and Kraft. FMS, Y-Balance and asymmetries remain
separate trainer observations and must never reduce this score.

**Why:** Movement-quality observations are valuable for training decisions but
do not establish a proportional loss of sport performance or an individual
injury prediction. Missing performance modules also must not be treated as poor
performance.

**How to apply:** Only form a total from at least two score-capable modules and
normalise the published weights across those modules. Keep values without a
validated reference visible as measurements or hints, but do not manufacture a
score. In particular, the currently derived female Sprint/CMJ/COD thresholds
are not score-capable until validated reference data is available.
